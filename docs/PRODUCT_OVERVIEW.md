# Product Overview

## Executive Summary
EduIQ is an Academic Intelligence Platform designed to unify education management for students, teachers, and administrators. By transforming disconnected academic records into explainable performance signals, EduIQ helps institutions recognize learning risks early and take targeted action.

---

## Value Proposition by Role

### 1. For Students: Clarity & Targeted Action
- **The Problem**: Students receive grades at the end of a term when it is too late to adjust study habits.
- **The Solution**: EduIQ provides continuous academic health feedback, highlighting weak subjects and offering actionable step-by-step study tips.

### 2. For Teachers: Educator Productivity & Classroom Insights
- **The Problem**: Teachers spend hours manually tracking attendance, grading papers, and identifying struggling students across multiple spreadsheets.
- **The Solution**: EduIQ streamlines attendance with one-click section markers, provides an AI Grading Assistant for rapid coursework evaluation, and surfaces classroom risk signals automatically.

### 3. For Administrators: Institutional Oversight & Governance
- **The Problem**: Academic leadership lacks macro-level visibility into departmental performance trends and course health.
- **The Solution**: EduIQ delivers system-wide analytics, faculty roster management, and printable official transcripts and evaluation summaries.
