# User Guide: Getting Started with EduIQ

Welcome to EduIQ! This guide will help non-technical users and hackathon judges explore the platform step-by-step.

---

## How to Access the Platform

### Step 1: Launch the Application
Open your browser and navigate to the local environment (e.g. `http://localhost:5178/`).

### Step 2: Choose Your Role
Click **Get started →** or **Sign in** in the top navigation bar. In the login view, select one of the three pre-configured demo profiles:
- **Student**: Maya Whitfield (`maya@eduiq.edu`)
- **Teacher**: Dr. Elena Marsh (`elena@eduiq.edu`)
- **Administrator**: System Admin (`admin@eduiq.edu`)

Click **Continue &rarr;** to enter your workspace.

---

## Exploring the Workspaces

### 🎓 Student Workflow
1. **Dashboard**: View your overall composite academic score (e.g. `73%`), attendance percentage ring, and recent activity.
2. **My Courses**: Browse enrolled coursework and click any course card to inspect its syllabus and instructor info.
3. **Assignments**: View upcoming coursework deadlines and click **Submit** to upload your work.
4. **Examinations**: Take an interactive online test with a live countdown timer and instant evaluation.
5. **My Progress**: Inspect weak subject warnings and AI-generated study recommendations.

### 👩‍🏫 Teacher Workflow
1. **Educator Control Center**: Review section schedules and upcoming tasks.
2. **Attendance Portal**: Select a section and quickly toggle students between **Present**, **Late**, and **Absent**, or click **Mark All Present**.
3. **Submissions & AI Grading**: Open a submitted assignment, review the document preview, click **[Accept AI Score]**, and save grades.
4. **Examinations**: Build new MCQ assessments using the MCQ Builder modal.
5. **Academic Insights**: View the Academic Pulse node diagram to see which students need support.

### 🏛️ Administrator Workflow
1. **Institutional Governance**: Manage student rosters, faculty assignments, and course catalogs.
2. **Analytics**: Monitor institutional grade distributions and attendance health across departments.
3. **Official Reports**: Select a student and click **Print Report** to review or print official academic evaluation transcripts.

---

## Useful Shortcuts & Help Controls
- **Command Palette**: Press `⌘K` (or `Ctrl + K`) anywhere in the workspace to search students, courses, or navigate instantly.
- **AI Copilot**: Click the **AI Copilot** button in the topbar to open the contextual assistant drawer.
- **Product Intro & Help**: Click the `?` icon in the topbar anytime to replay the guided introduction or read quick role-specific tips.
